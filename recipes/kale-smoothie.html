Antioxidant Explosion

Description

The antioxidant explosion smoothie is a delicious smoothie featuring lots of micronutrients! It is sweet, but still very healthy. It can be enjoyed at any time of the day, but I think it is best after a hard run.

Ingredients

  • Around 3 cups of water
  • 1/2 cup frozen blueberries
  • A small chunk of fresh ginger root
  • About 5 large kale leaves
  • 1 Lemon
  • A small mint leaf (optional)

Steps

  1. Put the water in your blender
  2. Place the rest of the ingredients in the blender and blend until smooth, about 1:30.
  3. Serve with an optional mint leaf on top
